"Generate a detailed website structure (sitemap) and key content points for a new website for a professional catering business. The primary goals of the website are to attract potential clients (both corporate and private events like weddings and parties), showcase the quality and services offered, build trust, and generate qualified leads through quote requests and contact forms.

The website design and content must strongly feature and be built around one central Unique Selling Proposition (USP). Choose one of the following USPs to focus on for this website outline:

Hyper-Local & Seasonal Sourcing: Emphasizing fresh, locally sourced ingredients and seasonal menus.
Niche Specialization: Focusing exclusively on being the expert provider for [Choose ONE specific niche: e.g., Corporate Luncheons, Elegant Weddings, Vegan Gourmet Events].
Unmatched Customization & Personal Service: Highlighting bespoke menu creation and dedicated client service.
Seamless Digital Experience & Transparent Pricing: Focusing on easy online quotes/ordering and clear, upfront costs.
Unique Culinary Focus: Showcasing a distinct signature style [Choose ONE specific style: e.g., Modern Indian Fusion, Authentic Italian Cuisine, Live Fire Cooking Experiences].
The website structure and content outline should include the following essential elements:

Homepage: Engaging introduction, clear statement of the chosen USP, high-quality visuals, highlights of services, testimonials snippets, clear CTAs.
About Us / Our Story: Detail the caterer's background, mission, values, experience, and team. Reinforce the chosen USP narrative here.
Services: Clearly describe the types of events catered (e.g., Corporate, Weddings, Private Parties) and the levels of service offered (e.g., Drop-off, Buffet Setup, Full Service with Staff). Tailor descriptions based on the chosen USP (e.g., emphasize corporate efficiency if USP #2 is Corporate).
Menus: Showcase sample menus (potentially categorized by event type or season if USP #1). Must explicitly mention customization options (especially if USP #3). Include high-quality food photos. Address how pricing is presented (especially if USP #4). Consider downloadable PDF options.
Gallery / Portfolio: A visually rich section with high-resolution photos and/or videos from past events. Filterable by event type if applicable. Photos should align with the USP (e.g., show local ingredients if USP #1, elegant weddings if USP #2).
Testimonials / Our Clients: Dedicated page for client reviews, quotes, and potentially video testimonials. Include logos of corporate clients if applicable. Testimonials should ideally reinforce the USP.
Contact Us / Request a Quote: Prominent contact information (phone, email). A detailed inquiry form designed to gather essential information efficiently (event type, date, guest count, venue, budget range, dietary needs, specific requests). The form's detail level might vary slightly based on the USP (e.g., more customization fields for USP #3).
(Optional but Recommended) Blog: Suggest potential blog categories or topics relevant to the chosen USP and catering in general (e.g., seasonal ingredient spotlights, event planning tips, featured events).
General Requirements:
Visuals: Emphasize the need for abundant, professional, high-quality photography and videography throughout the site.
Navigation: Ensure clear, intuitive site navigation.
Call-to-Actions (CTAs): Strategically place CTAs like "Get a Quote," "View Menus," "Contact Us" on relevant pages.
Trust Factors: Integrate elements that build credibility (experience, client logos, testimonials, clear information).
Mobile Responsiveness: The design and layout must be fully responsive for all devices.
Tone: Professional, inviting, delicious, trustworthy, and reflective of the chosen USP.
Provide a potential sitemap and bullet points outlining the key information, messaging, and visual focus for each page, ensuring the chosen USP is woven throughout consistently."
